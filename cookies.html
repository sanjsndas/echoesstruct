Cookies Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies on Echoes Of Structure.

Last updated: 15 March 2025

This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them on Echoes Of Structure (the "Journal", "we", "us", or "our"), and the choices you have regarding their use. By continuing to browse this website, you agree to our use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners. Cookies do not typically contain any information that personally identifies a user, but personal information that we store about you may be linked to the information stored in and obtained from cookies.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for a variety of reasons, including:

  • Essential functionality: To ensure the website functions properly — for example, remembering your cookie preferences or enabling basic navigation.
  • Analytics and performance: To understand how visitors interact with the Journal, which pages are most popular, and how we can improve the reading experience.
  • Preferences: To remember settings you have chosen, such as language or display preferences.
  • Advertising and partnerships: In limited cases, we may use cookies to deliver relevant editorial partnerships or sponsored content, always respecting your privacy choices.

3. Types of cookies we use

The table below explains the specific cookies we use and their purpose:

Cookie name / type Purpose Duration
eos_session (essential) Maintains your session while you browse the site; essential for the website to function. Session
eos_preferences Stores your cookie consent choices and display preferences (e.g., dark mode if applicable). 12 months
_ga, _gid (Google Analytics) Used to collect anonymous statistics about how visitors use the site, such as pages visited and time on page. 2 years / 24 hours
eos_newsletter Remembers if you have dismissed the newsletter signup prompt. 6 months
Third-party (embedded content) Embedded content from platforms such as Vimeo, YouTube, or social media may set their own cookies when you interact with them. Varies by provider

We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information, and we never sell your data to third parties.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. For example, we use Google Analytics to help us understand site traffic. These third parties may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect information about your use of our site and other websites. We do not control these third-party cookies — please refer to the respective privacy policies of these providers for more information.

5. Your choices regarding cookies

You have several options when it comes to managing cookies:

  • Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. You can typically set your browser to refuse cookies or delete certain cookies. Please note that if you disable essential cookies, parts of the website may not function properly.
  • Opt-out of analytics: You can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to prevent your data from being used by Google Analytics.
  • Cookie consent banner: Where available, you can manage your preferences via our cookie consent tool.

To find out more about cookies, including how to see what cookies have been set and how to manage and delete them, visit www.aboutcookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org.

6. Changes to this Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
