Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept guest articles or pitches? +

Yes — we welcome well-researched pitches from architects, designers, and writers. Please send a short synopsis (200–300 words) and a link to previous work to pitches@echoesofstructure.com.

How can I advertise or partner with Echoes Of Structure? +

We offer limited, carefully curated partnerships. For media kits and rates, please email partners@echoesofstructure.com with a brief introduction.

I have a question about an article — who should I contact? +

For corrections, clarifications, or general feedback on published pieces, email editorial@echoesofstructure.com.
