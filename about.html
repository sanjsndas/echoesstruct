About Echoes Of Structure

An independent journal exploring the intersection of interior design, architecture, and the human experience of space — through essays, guides, and critical perspectives.

Our Mission

Echoes Of Structure was founded on a simple belief: the spaces we inhabit shape how we think, feel, and live. Yet too often, design writing is either overly technical or hopelessly superficial. We aim to bridge that gap — offering rigorous, accessible journalism that treats interiors and architecture as cultural artefacts worthy of serious attention.

We publish in-depth articles, field guides, and long-form essays on interior design, architecture, materials, lighting, space planning, and spatial theory. Our contributors include practising architects, designers, historians, and writers who bring both expertise and curiosity to their work.

"The most radical act in design today may be restraint — creating rooms that breathe rather than shout."

— Elena Marchetti, Founding Editor

What We Cover

Our editorial scope is intentionally broad, but always grounded in the physical and the sensory. We are less interested in trends than in enduring principles. We ask: how does light change a room? Why do certain materials age gracefully? What makes a floor plan feel generous rather than mean? How do cultural traditions inform contemporary practice?

We publish across six core categories: Interior Design, Architecture, Materials, Space Planning, Lighting, and Furniture. Within each, we look for stories that reveal something new — whether through historical research, technical analysis, or direct conversations with the people shaping the built environment.

Editorial Values

  • Clarity over jargon. Good design writing should be intelligible to the interested layperson and still valuable to the professional. We avoid unnecessary obscurity.
  • Depth over speed. We take time with our articles. Many of our long reads involve weeks of research, interviews, and revision. We prioritise substance over the news cycle.
  • Independence. We are not funded by advertisers or sponsored content. Our recommendations and critiques are entirely our own.
  • Sustainability as default. We treat environmental responsibility not as a niche concern but as a fundamental lens through which all design should be evaluated.
  • Global perspective. Our contributors write from Europe, Asia, North America, and beyond, reflecting the rich diversity of architectural and interior traditions worldwide.

Work With Us

We are always interested in hearing from writers, photographers, and designers who share our approach. If you have a story idea, a portfolio, or a pitch, please get in touch. We welcome contributions from both established voices and emerging talents.

For general enquiries, including media requests and partnership opportunities, please contact us at hello@echoesofstructure.com.

A Note on Advertising

Echoes Of Structure does not accept sponsored posts, native advertising, or paid product placement. Our editorial content is entirely independent. We believe that trust is the most valuable currency a publication can have, and we protect it fiercely.
